# AI Intensive Bootcamp

Source basis: current public pages on aiforindonesia.com, reviewed 2026-10-02.

## Audience and purpose
AI Intensive Bootcamp is an intensive learning program aimed at developing
skills for AI-related careers. Public materials indicate it is suitable for
people from IT and non-IT backgrounds, including fresh graduates,
professionals, career switchers, students/academics, and people upskilling.

The specialist pages state that participants do not need prior IT/programming
experience because Bootcamp Preparation teaches Python from zero.
