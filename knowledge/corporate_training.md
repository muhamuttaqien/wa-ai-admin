# AI Corporate Training

Source basis: https://aiforindonesia.com/corporate-training/
Reviewed 2026-10-02.

## Purpose
Corporate Training is designed to develop AI technology skills and literacy
for employees, supporting innovation, operational efficiency, and business
competitiveness.

Training combines theory and practice, real business case studies, and
post-training support/consultation.

## Program types
1. Generative AI for Business
   - Intended for employees/non-technical teams using AI tools for automation
     and work-efficiency improvement.
   - Topics presented on the website include AI and Generative AI fundamentals,
     tools such as ChatGPT, Gemini and Copilot, prompting, and business use
     cases such as planning, sales, recruitment, finance, and others.

2. AI for Engineering
   - Intended for technical teams.
   - Topics include Python and AI fundamentals, prediction/model development,
     system optimization, integration, deployment, and model monitoring.
   - The FAQ also describes deeper Generative AI implementation such as
     fine-tuning, integration, and deployment.

3. Custom AI & Data Training
   - Material can be adjusted to participant needs and skill level.
   - Focus can be tailored to specific industry needs and business challenges.

## Delivery
Available options:
- Online via Zoom or Google Meet
- Onsite at the client's office
- Hybrid

The website describes scheduling/time as flexible.

## Curriculum and learning
- Curriculum can be customized to company needs.
- Training includes interactive exercises, demonstrations, hands-on AI labs,
  and business case studies.
- Trainers are described as experienced AI practitioners.
- Website states material access is lifetime access.
- Website states 2–3 trained facilitators.
- Learning method can be online, offline, project-based, case-study based, etc.

## Certificate and support
- Participants who complete Corporate Training receive an official certificate.
- Post-training support and consultation are available to support AI
  implementation in the business.

## Participants
- No minimum or maximum participant count is stated for Corporate Training.

## Pricing and payment
- The website does NOT publish a fixed Corporate Training price.
- It states that program cost is calculated based on the number of training
  sessions and can be adjusted to company needs.
- Term of Payment (ToP) can be discussed according to company needs/policies.

Do not invent a Corporate Training price or quotation.
If asked for an exact quotation, explain that it depends on the training
requirements/sessions and needs confirmation/discussion with the team.
