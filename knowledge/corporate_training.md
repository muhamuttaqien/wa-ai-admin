# AI Corporate Training

Source basis: current official AI Corporate Training page on aiforindonesia.com, reviewed 2026-10-03.

## Program overview
AI Corporate Training is Indonesia AI's company training program for developing employee AI and data skills and AI literacy. The program is designed to support business innovation, operational efficiency, workforce capability, and competitiveness in the digital era.

Training can combine theory, hands-on practice, interactive exercises, demonstrations, real business case studies, and post-training support/consultation.

## Primary use cases
Corporate Training can support needs such as:
- employee AI upskilling
- AI literacy across teams/divisions
- onboarding AI knowledge for new employees
- productivity improvement using AI tools
- technical AI capability development
- AI/data training for specific industry or business problems
- cross-functional understanding of AI

The website describes AI for Business use cases across functions such as marketing, production, finance, product development, customer service, planning, sales, and recruitment.

## Training for technical and non-technical teams
Corporate Training is available for both technical and non-technical audiences.

The three main program families are:
- Generative AI for Business
- AI for Engineering
- Custom AI & Data Training

## Generative AI for Business
### Audience
Primarily relevant for non-technical/business teams that want to use Generative AI tools to improve productivity, automate parts of work, and understand practical AI use cases.

### Topics represented on the website
- fundamentals of AI and Generative AI
- application of Generative AI in companies
- AI tools such as ChatGPT, Gemini, and Copilot
- prompting / prompt techniques
- practical business use cases
- planning
- sales
- recruitment
- finance
- other company workflows according to needs

The curriculum can be adjusted to the company's context rather than treated as one rigid syllabus.

## AI for Engineering
### Audience
Relevant for technical teams that want deeper AI implementation and engineering capability.

### Topics represented on the website
- Python Programming
- AI fundamentals
- building prediction models
- AI-based system optimization
- model/system integration
- deployment
- model monitoring
- implementation best practices

The FAQ also describes technical training needs that may include deeper Generative AI implementation such as model fine-tuning, integration, and deployment.

Do not promise that every AI for Engineering engagement automatically includes every topic above. Final curriculum depends on the agreed company needs.

## Custom AI & Data Training
Custom AI & Data Training is intended for organizations that need a curriculum adapted to their specific context.

It can be adjusted based on:
- business objectives
- participant knowledge level
- technical/non-technical audience
- industry needs
- company-specific AI/data challenges
- desired practical outcomes

The program can focus on AI solutions that participants can relate directly to their work and business environment.

## Onboarding AI for New Employee
The website also positions AI training as an onboarding option for new employees, helping align AI understanding across divisions so employees can adapt more quickly and work more effectively with a common AI foundation.

## Delivery options
Three delivery modes are available:

### Online Training
- delivered through Zoom or Google Meet
- participants receive access to relevant learning materials

### Onsite Training
- conducted directly at the client's/company's location
- suitable when the company prefers in-person interaction

### Hybrid Training
- combination of online and onsite delivery
- intended to provide greater flexibility according to company needs

Training time/scheduling is described as flexible and can be discussed according to company requirements.

## Curriculum customization
The Corporate Training curriculum can be customized according to company needs.

Customization can consider:
- target business outcome
- participant roles/divisions
- current participant skill level
- preferred AI tools or technical depth
- industry context
- desired exercises or business case studies
- number and structure of training sessions

The AI Admin should gather the company's goal before assuming which curriculum is most suitable.

## Learning method
Learning methods can include:
- theory
- hands-on AI labs
- interactive exercises
- live demonstrations
- real business case studies
- project-based learning
- online learning
- onsite learning
- customized practical exercises

The website emphasizes practical learning and real business use cases rather than lecture-only delivery.

## Trainers and facilitators
- Trainers are described as experienced AI practitioners/professionals with industry experience.
- The website states 2–3 trained facilitators.

Do not promise a specific named trainer or facilitator until the assignment is confirmed for the engagement.

## Learning materials
The website states that participants receive lifetime access to training materials.

Do not invent the exact learning platform, file format, recording availability, or material package for a specific corporate engagement unless it has been confirmed.

## Certificate
Participants who complete Corporate Training receive an official certificate as evidence of participation/completion.

Do not invent certificate wording, accreditation, SKP/credit status, or external certification recognition unless explicitly confirmed.

## Post-training support
Indonesia AI provides post-training support and consultation intended to help companies with AI implementation after training.

This can be described as continued support/consultation, but do not invent a fixed support duration, SLA, number of consultation hours, or guaranteed implementation outcome unless agreed in the proposal.

## Participant count
There is no stated minimum or maximum participant count for Corporate Training.

Do not reject a company merely because its participant count is small or large. Collect the expected number of participants because it is important for training design and proposal preparation.

## Pricing
There is no fixed public Corporate Training price.

The website states that program cost is calculated based on the number of training sessions and can be adjusted according to company needs.

Other requirements can also affect the final proposal because the program itself is customizable.

Never invent an exact quotation, per-person price, discount, or package price.

If the customer asks for an exact price, explain naturally that the proposal is adjusted to the training needs and number of sessions, then gather/confirm the relevant requirements for the team.

## Payment and Term of Payment
Indonesia AI is open to discussing Term of Payment (ToP) according to the company's needs and policies.

Do not invent specific payment milestones, percentages, invoice deadlines, or credit terms unless they have been agreed/confirmed.

## Information useful for preparing a proposal
For B2B leads, the following information is useful and is already aligned with the conversation flow:
- contact name
- company/organization name
- representative division
- estimated participant count
- preferred delivery mode, online / onsite / hybrid
- main training goal
- whether the need is closer to Generative AI for Business, AI for Engineering, or Custom AI & Data Training

Additional context such as business challenges, desired outcomes, participant background, schedule preferences, or budget can help proposal preparation when volunteered or needed, but should not make the conversation feel like excessive qualification.

## Corporate Training positioning
Useful factual differentiators stated on the website include:
- customizable curriculum
- experienced AI practitioners
- hands-on AI labs
- real business case studies
- flexible learning methods
- lifetime material access
- 2–3 trained facilitators
- post-training support and consultation
- online, onsite, and hybrid options

Present these as program features, not as unsupported claims that Indonesia AI is objectively better than every alternative.

## Organization and credibility facts
The website identifies the organization as PT. Teknologi Artifisial Indonesia.

The Corporate Training page also presents company recognition including participation/recognition in programs associated with IndigoSpace Telkom Indonesia, 1000 Startup Digital / KOMINFO, Founderplus/FundEx, and Digital Leadership Academy. These may be mentioned when relevant to a customer asking about company credibility, but should not dominate ordinary training conversations.

- Answer known Corporate Training facts directly and professionally as Indonesia AI's admin.
- Do not say "berdasarkan yang tercantum", "seperti yang tercantum", "tercantum di website", or similar source-reading language in customer-facing replies.
